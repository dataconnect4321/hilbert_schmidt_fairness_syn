Fidelity - how close is the synthetic data to the real data?

Numeric Wasserstein-1 measures the average "cost" of moving the synthetic numeric distribution onto the real one, normalized by each column's standard deviation. 0 = identical distributions.

Categorical total variation (TV) is the fraction of categorical records that would need to change category for the synthetic frequency distribution to exactly match the real one. 0 = identical.

Correlation-matrix delta is the average absolute difference between the real and synthetic numeric correlation matrices - it checks whether relationships between columns (not just each column on its own) survived generation.

Privacy - could synthetic records leak real training data?

DCR ratio (distance-to-closest-record) compares how close synthetic records sit to the real training data versus how close a held-out real record sits to that same training data. A ratio near or above 1.0 means synthetic records are no closer to individual training records than other real records naturally are - i.e. no evidence of memorization. A ratio well below 0.5 would suggest the model copied training examples rather than generalizing.

DP guarantee (epsilon, delta) is the formal differential-privacy bound from DP-SGD training, tracked by a Renyi-DP accountant across every training step. It holds regardless of what any attacker does with the trained model - unlike DCR, which is only an empirical check on this one generated batch.

DCR ratio0.6691 (Moderate)
DP guarantee0.52

A lower epsilon is a stronger guarantee; 0.52 at delta=1e-05 is a typical research-grade budget.

Fairness - does the synthetic data reduce group bias?

Each protected group's loan origination rate is shown below for the real data and the FLIP-generated synthetic data. The demographic parity (DP) difference is the gap between the highest- and lowest-rate group; the DP ratio is the lowest rate divided by the highest. The widely used four-fifths rule treats a ratio below 0.80 as evidence of disparate impact.

FLIP's disentanglement objective (CKA-based, Sec. 4.2-4.3 of the paper) pushes the VAE's latent and reconstructed representations toward statistical independence from the protected attribute during Phase 2 training, so the synthetic data's group gap should shrink relative to the real data's.
